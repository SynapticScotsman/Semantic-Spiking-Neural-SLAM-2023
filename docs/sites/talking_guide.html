01 ·

The frame — what this project actually claims

A Vector Symbolic Architecture memory: every observation is bound (element-wise complex multiply) to a grid-cell-style position code and added into one 4096-dimensional phasor vector. The map never grows; updating is one addition; merging two robots' maps is one addition; and unbinding a concept renders a belief field over the whole floor. We are not claiming better accuracy than the state of the art — we claim a different, measured trade: a memory ~1000× smaller that reaches the accuracy ceiling of its own perception, with capabilities (exact merge, one algebra for what/where/when) that no established map representation has.

say this

"VSAs are the algebraic composition layer for robotic memory: we buy constant size, one-add updates and exact merging, and we measured that it costs essentially nothing against the perception we feed it."

04 ·

The findings, in the order we earned them

  • arc 1 · treatments
    Centre, don't whiten — scoped.

    Mean-removal carries 40–83% of whitening's benefit depending on encoder (12% for EigenPlaces); whitening was catastrophic on the classroom (−47.6% signal, 5 seeds) yet neutral on chess. Claims are scoped per encoder × scene, never universal.

  • arc 2 · the capacity law
    χ(N) = aμN + √N·√(α/PR + γ/hd)·h(N,τ).

    Mean term exactly linear to 3×10⁵; two-source quadrature prefactor predicted a held-out cell to 1.4% and explains the encoder-independent whitened floor; temporal persistence coherent for N≲τ then √-reverting to √(1+τ/τ₀). The mean term is the τ→∞ limit — which unifies why centring and key-diversity (yaw binding) are interchangeable rescues.

  • arc 3 · the yaw/key law
    Content conditioning and key diversity are substitutes.

    Bind-yaw rescued ill-conditioned crops on TUM, became harmful with viewpoint-invariant EigenPlaces on the classroom (0.35→1.00 m), then mildly helped chess's orbit tail — trajectory-dependent, exactly as the law predicts.

  • arc 4 · the frontend verdict
    Paul's hypothesis confirmed: the descriptor was the bottleneck.

    EigenPlaces lifted the classroom kNN ceiling 0.78→0.43 m and the VSA followed to 0.35 m (seed-0) — the memory tracks its ceiling; and raw-vs-z-scored demonstrated the mean-term mechanism live on a brand-new encoder.

  • arc 5 · dual granularity
    The 2×2 double dissociation, all four cells measured.

    Object task: crops 0.769 vs place-vectors 0.429 (chance 0.312). Place task: crops 0.96 m vs EigenPlaces 0.35 m. Each representation wins its own task — "granularity matches query" is measured, and both coexist in one trace.

  • arc 6 · public benchmarks
    7-Scenes chess + all 8 Replica scenes.

    Chess: 0.36 m median 3D at 32 KB (ceiling 0.29 m; PQ cannot exist below its 2 MB codebook). Replica: grounding at the perception ceiling on every scene, zero memory-attributable misses, bounded per-class insertion fixing the imbalance failure the law predicted (44→62% on the internal diagnostic).

  • arc 7 · map joining
    Merge = addition beats merge = association, measured.

    8 scenes × 4 robots: VSA addition 89% mean (worst 80%), zero association decisions, 10⁻¹² exact; the scene-graph-style associative merge 84% (worst 67%) after 622 decisions. Survey: bundling as a between-robot map-merge operator appears to be novel.

  • arc 8 · the metric correction
    Paul's standing rule: no invented metrics.

    Our grounding "truth-R@1 @ 1 m" was a bespoke protocol → demoted to internal diagnostic. The student package now scores both systems under ConceptGraphs' own mAcc/F-mIoU with their scorer — including the pre-registered honest expectation that an object memory scores low when walls/floor dominate the metric.

06 ·

The theory column — a law that predicts, not decorates

Three verified predictions: (1) the quadrature prefactor √(α/PR + γ/hd) hit a held-out synthetic cell to 1.4%; (2) it retro-explains the encoder-independent whitened floor measured months earlier (that's the γ/hd projection-noise term — no treatment can remove it, only more dimensions); (3) it forecast the class-imbalance failure on Replica (couch×1150 drowning book×5 is the mean term at class level) and the fix's direction (bounded insertion). Persistence: correlated frames add coherently for N≲τ then √-revert to a permanent √(1+τ/τ₀) elevation — the mean term is the τ→∞ limit, unifying the two rescue mechanisms.

say this

"We can tell you before deployment how much a given encoder, scene and duration will crosstalk — and the law has already called two real failures in advance."

07 ·

The merge story — the paper's sharpest edge

Every established family merges by inference: occupancy grids search for a transform, pose graphs need loop closures + NP-hard outlier rejection + distributed optimisation, scene graphs add semantic node association on top, neural maps run consensus optimisation over weights. All produce estimates; none are order-independent. Ours is a commutative, associative addition — bit-identical to the jointly-built map, and the literature survey found no prior use of bundling as the between-robot map-merge operator. The honest caveat rides on every claim: a shared coordinate frame is assumed, not solved — frame recovery by correlation sweep exists in the repo, its robustness study is queued.

say this

"Their merge is an algorithm; ours is an arithmetic operation. We measured what that's worth: the association-based merge lost 16 points on its worst scene to wrong matches; ours cannot make a wrong match."

08 ·

What we do not claim — lead with these before anyone asks

  • Not SOTA accuracy, anywhere. DSAC* is 10× better at relocalisation; SAM pipelines label scenes we cannot. Our claim is the trade, not the crown.
  • Perception-bound. Every measured miss is the frontend's (mislabels, missed detections, depth-through-glass). The memory adds ~nothing — and fixes nothing.
  • Vocabulary-bound. COCO-80 sees 9–31% of Replica's GT instances; open-vocab frontends are the extension, partially built (CLIP relabeling).
  • Shared frame assumed. Alignment remains the classical problem.
  • Bounded capacity, characterised. Beyond the law's crossover, growing databases win on accuracy (measured: kNN overtakes at N≈800 on class retrieval).
  • Seed honesty. Single-split headline numbers carry their seed tags (0.35 m seed-0 vs 0.84 m 5-seed median, always together).
