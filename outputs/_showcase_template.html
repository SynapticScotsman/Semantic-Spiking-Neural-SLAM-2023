00 ·

The whole trick, in one line of algebra

See something → bind what it looks like to where (and when) you are → add it into the one vector you own. To ask a question, unbind the thing you're asking about — what remains is a heat-map over every place it was seen. No database, no graph, no optimiser: a 4096-dimensional complex vector and three operations.

the same algebra holds place ⊗ position for localisation and content ⊗ time for episodic recall — one memory, three query types

05 ·

The law underneath — why it works and when it stops

Crosstalk χ(N) measured on controlled synthetic geometry to a million items (real sweep data below). The mean term never bends — kill it or it kills you (centring, or key diversity). Clean content rides the √N floor. Temporal persistence adds a bump that √-reverts. The quadrature prefactor √(α/PR + γ/hd) predicted a held-out cell to 1.4% — this law also predicted the class-imbalance failure in §02 and its fix (bounded insertion, 44→62% provisional R@1).

06 ·

What we do not claim

  • Geometry keeps the precision crown. DSAC*-class relocalisers are ~10× more accurate where per-scene GPU training is acceptable. Different tool, different regime.
  • Accuracy is perception-bound. The trace reaches its frontend's ceiling and stops there; it cannot repair a detector's stable mistakes (the vase above).
  • The vocabulary bounds the battery. COCO-80 sees 9–31% of each scene's GT instances; open-vocabulary frontends are the extension path.
  • Merging assumes a shared frame. The algebra does not solve alignment — that remains the classical problem; our correlation-sweep recovery awaits its robustness study.
  • Capacity is bounded and characterised. The §04 law says when crosstalk bites; beyond it, growing databases win on accuracy. Constant memory is the point, not a free lunch.

A fixed-size vector memory that reaches the accuracy ceiling of its own perception on public benchmarks — while being ~1000× smaller than the database it replaces, updating in one addition, merging bit-exactly with zero data association, and answering where, what and when in a single algebra.
